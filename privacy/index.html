Privacy

How yup.studio handles personal data when you visit our website, send an enquiry or use our services. Effective 31 October 2026.

Controller and contact

For the yup.studio website, enquiries and business relationships, the controller is Alena Kuřítka, trading as yup.studio, Czech business ID 87169151, registered office V Horkách 1730/3, 140 00 Praha 4 – Nusle, registered in the Czech Trade Licensing Register. Contact for questions and data rights: hello@yup.studio.

What information we process and why

For enquiries we process your name, email, optional phone, business name or website link, and message to respond and prepare an offer. For customer contracts we handle necessary identity and contact details, correspondence, orders, payment and accounting records. IP addresses, operational logs and essential technical identifiers may also be processed to secure the service.

Legal bases

Requests concerning your own potential contract are handled under GDPR Article 6(1)(b); correspondence with business representatives and general business enquiries under legitimate interests in communication (Article 6(1)(f)). Contracts are performed under (b), legal and accounting duties under (c), and security, abuse prevention and protection of legal claims under (f). Supplying an email address does not constitute consent to advertising; electronic marketing is sent only where separate legal requirements permit it.

Contact form delivery

Messages submitted through our contact form are processed through Cloudflare Workers and delivered to the email mailbox used by yup.studio. Correspondence may remain in the mailbox, replies and necessary operational records. We do not promise a separate, persistent enquiry database. A name and email are needed to reply; phone and website are optional.

Client websites, bookings and galleries

For contact forms, bookings or galleries on a client website, that website's operator is generally the controller and yup.studio acts as technical processor under agreed terms. Booking information may be held in Google Calendar, Cloudflare Durable Objects and email notifications. Cancelling a booking does not automatically erase every copy, log or email. Visitors should primarily contact the operator of that website to exercise their rights; yup.studio will assist the operator.

Recipients and international transfers

Depending on features used, service providers may include Cloudflare (website services, forms and email infrastructure), Google (mailbox and possibly calendars), GitHub (GitHub Pages and website operations) and Stripe when you visit an external payment page. Roles vary by service; Stripe also provides its own privacy notice for payment processing. Certain services may involve transfers outside the EEA; applicable GDPR mechanisms, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses where relevant, are required. You can request further details about providers used.

Retention

We keep enquiries and correspondence while handling them and afterward only for the time reasonably needed to document communications or protect legal rights in the particular case. Unsuccessful enquiries are not retained indefinitely for marketing. Contract and accounting data are retained for the service term and any applicable legal record-keeping or claim-protection period. Technical data are retained as necessary for security and diagnosis, including providers' applicable retention practices. Retention and deletion for client-site bookings are agreed with the controller for the active features; cancellation alone is not automatic erasure. You can request deletion, subject to lawful retention obligations.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and where applicable data portability. You may withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. Contact hello@yup.studio; we respond within GDPR time limits, normally one month. You may complain to the Czech Data Protection Authority (uoou.gov.cz).

Cookies and external services

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies by default. Essential technical storage and third-party services may be used for operation and abuse prevention. Any future analytics or marketing technology requiring consent will not be activated without appropriate consent. External payment links lead to third-party services governed by their own notices.

Effective date and updates

This notice applies to yup.studio from 31 October 2026. We will update it when processing purposes, recipients or technologies change. Statutory rights and obligations remain unaffected.
